## Long description

Strap on jet boots and fight across 99 hand-built arenas in a fast side-view shooter
where movement is everything. Bunny-hop to keep your speed, burn fuel to cross gaps,
roll under fire, and throw grenades that bounce the way you expect.

**Every mode, every map, with or without friends.** Deathmatch, Teammatch, Capture the
Flag, Infiltration, Hold the Flag, Rambomatch, Pointmatch, Domination, Battle Royale and
Gun Game, plus Realistic, Survival and Advance rule sets. Bots play every objective on
every map: they grab flags, defend points, pick up medikits and call things out on the radio.

**Vehicles.** Two-seat buggies race along the long maps: one of you drives, the other works
the mounted machine gun. On the widest maps, armored tanks lob high-explosive shells and
shrug off rifle fire, so bring a LAW. Bots drive both.

**Fifteen weapons and the tools around them.** Deagles to Minigun, Barrett to flamethrower,
knife, chainsaw, LAW, frag and cluster grenades, fixed M2 guns, and power-ups (Predator,
Berserker, Vest, Cluster). Everything on the map is labelled, so you always know what you're picking up.

**Online.** Host a game or run a dedicated server. Find games on your network or through a
master server, with live ping and player counts. Quick Join drops you into the best open
match, and if your connection drops you rejoin with your name, score and team.

**Make your own.** An in-game map editor (platforms, spawns, flags, kits, vehicles), a seeded
random map generator, full key / gamepad / touch rebinding, and cosmetics.

## Content rating notes (IARC / ESRB-style questionnaire)
- Realistic-ish violence toward humans, blood and gibs (the Blood slider in Settings goes to 0).
- No gambling, no purchases, no user-to-user money. In-game text chat online (host can kick / vote-kick).
- No data collected; the game only talks to the hosts you join and an optional master server.

## System requirements (conservative; confirm on real hardware)

- **Android:** Android 8+, 3 GB RAM, OpenGL ES 3.0 GPU.
