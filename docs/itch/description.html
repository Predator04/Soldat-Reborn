Jetpacks. Bunny hops. Ragdolls. Free.

Tracerfall is a fast 2D run-and-gun shooter in the spirit of the cult classic Soldat: jet boots, momentum you can feel, ragdoll gibs and sixteen weapons, rebuilt for today's PCs and phones. Free, no ads, no microtransactions, and the code is open source.

Jump in with one click. QUICK PLAY drops you into the best online game, or a bot match if nobody's on. The official server runs 24/7 and fills empty slots with bots, so there's always a fight.

Features

  • Soldat-style physics — jet boots with fuel, bunny-hop momentum, prone, rolls, grenade cooking and weapon throws, ragdolls and gibs.
  • 10 modes — Capture the Flag, Deathmatch, Teammatch, Infiltration, Hold the Flag, Rambomatch, Pointmatch, Domination, Battle Royale and Gun Game, plus Realistic, Survival and Advance rules.
  • Bots everywhere — smart bots in every mode and on every map, including the levels players build: they climb, jet across gaps, capture flags and drive vehicles. Play offline, or let them keep servers full.
  • Maker mode — build a level like Mario Maker: drag ground, ramps, bridges, spikes, lava, jump pads, flags, weapons and vehicles onto the map; touching pieces melt into smooth terrain in the theme you pick. One button to PLAY, one to jump back into EDIT. Works on phones too.
  • LEVELS — browse, play, like and bookmark levels other players made (Hot, New, Most played, Top liked), publish your own and share its code.
  • Ultra HD — redrawn soldiers with a real jetpack, guns painted at 6x detail, HD terrain with twelve materials, and dynamic lighting from muzzle flashes, explosions and rockets.
  • Vehicles — buggies, tanks and helicopters with pilot and gunner seats, plus mounted M2 guns.
  • 100+ maps — the classic Soldat maps, reworked, plus everything players publish.
  • Online — host with one click (the relay means no router setup), LAN games, friends list with one-click join, leaderboard and career ranks, achievements, unlockable looks, kill cam and match replays.
  • Windows + Android — mouse and keyboard, gamepad, or touch controls; phones and PCs play on the same servers.
  • Free and open source — MIT-licensed code on GitHub. Bug reports and ideas welcome in the comments.

Controls (PC)

A / D move · W jump · right mouse jetpack · left mouse shoot · 1–0 weapons · R reload · E grenade · F throw / use · Tab scores · H help. Everything is rebindable; gamepads work out of the box.

System requirements

Windows: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, any graphics card with OpenGL 3.3 (almost every PC from the last ten years). Low graphics mode runs on old laptops.
Android: Android 7 or newer.

Languages

English, Español, Português (Brasil), Deutsch, Français.
